Does Momentum Investing Generate Excess Returns in the Indian Stock Market?

A quantitative-finance research study examining whether systematic price momentum can generate statistically significant and economically meaningful excess returns in Indian equities.

Indian Equities6M / 12M MomentumWinner − LoserNifty 50 / Nifty 500Quantitative Finance
