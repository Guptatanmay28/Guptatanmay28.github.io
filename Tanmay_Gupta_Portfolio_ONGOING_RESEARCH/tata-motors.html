01
What the multiples suggest

FY26 valuation should be interpreted on the Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles consolidated continuing-operations basis. P/E, EV/EBITDA and balance-sheet measures should use the same reporting perimeter.

02
Relative conclusion

The peer set shows stronger profitability and more established earnings support at M&M and Maruti. Tata Motors PV therefore needs a recovery in margins and cash generation to justify a stronger relative valuation.

CALCULATION FRAMEWORK
Market Cap = Share Price × Shares Outstanding · EV = Market Cap + Debt − Cash · P/E = Price ÷ EPS · EV/EBITDA = EV ÷ EBITDA
